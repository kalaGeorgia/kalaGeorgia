Private Transfer from Tbilisi to Borjomi

Works well both as a standalone day trip (the park, the springs, a walk) and as the first leg of a longer route — many continue on to Akhaltsikhe or Vardzia.

About the route

Roughly 150 km, typically a 2–2.5 hour drive.

What is included

Pickup at your Tbilisi hotel or address, stops along the way as you like, water and chargers on board, help with luggage.

Vehicle

Toyota Alphard 2018, up to 6 passengers — comfortable for a family day trip with a child and a stroller.

Trip formats

A same-day round trip, a one-way transfer if you are staying overnight in Borjomi, or a combined route on to Akhaltsikhe or Vardzia by arrangement.

Pricing

Confirmed on WhatsApp for your date and trip format.
