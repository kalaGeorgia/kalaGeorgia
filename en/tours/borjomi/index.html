Private Borjomi Tour from Tbilisi

An easy day out of Tbilisi that can stay gentle or turn into a long one. The park and the springs alone make a relaxed trip; Rabati Castle and the Vardzia cave city are close enough to add if you want the drive to earn more.

Along the route

Borjomi Central Park with the mineral water springs and the cable car above the valley, and the forest edge of Borjomi-Kharagauli National Park. Rabati Castle in Akhaltsikhe and the Vardzia cave monastery lie further west and turn this into an extended day.

What is included

Pickup at your Tbilisi hotel, waiting while you walk the park, stops for lunch and photos, water and chargers on board.

Vehicle

Toyota Alphard 2018, up to 6 passengers — comfortable for a family day out, with room for a stroller and everything a day in a park needs.

Duration

The park on its own is a comfortable day, roughly 7–8 hours door to door. Adding Akhaltsikhe or Vardzia makes it a long one, closer to 11–12 hours with considerably more driving.

Pricing

Fixed for the day on WhatsApp, by which version of the route you choose. Park entrance and the cable car are paid on site.
