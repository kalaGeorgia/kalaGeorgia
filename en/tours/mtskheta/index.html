Private Mtskheta Tour from Tbilisi

The shortest of our tours and the easiest to fit into a full trip — Georgia’s ancient capital sits about half an hour from central Tbilisi, and half a day covers it without rushing.

Along the route

Jvari Monastery on the ridge above the confluence of the Mtkvari and Aragvi rivers, and Svetitskhoveli Cathedral in the old town below — both UNESCO World Heritage sites. Shio-Mgvime monastery in the gorge nearby can be added if you have the time.

What is included

Pickup at your Tbilisi hotel, driving between the sites, waiting while you walk around, water and chargers on board.

Vehicle

Toyota Alphard 2018, up to 6 passengers — a short drive, so this one works well with young children or older relatives.

Duration

Typically 4–5 hours door to door. Because the driving day is short, it is often paired with something else — Mtskheta sits on the road north, so Ananuri or Gudauri can follow the same afternoon.

Pricing

Fixed on WhatsApp. Half-day and full-day formats are both available — the full day is the one that adds a second destination.
